Loam Coffee

Packaging system and site for a roastery with three retail locations.

ClientLoam Coffee Roasters
Year2022
DisciplinePackaging design, web build

Project image

At a glance

  • PlatformWeb, print

The brief

Loam had a strong in-store identity but bags that changed with every new roast and no consistent system, plus a site that hadn't been touched since their first location opened.

What we did

We designed a packaging system flexible enough to handle a rotating single-origin lineup without a redesign every season, and rebuilt the site around the same visual language.

Result

The new packaging system has run for two years across more than thirty different roasts without a single one-off redesign.
