The brief

A two-person furniture workshop needed a brand and storefront that matched the quality of the work — something that didn't look like every other small-batch maker's site.

What we did

We built the identity around the material itself: the wood grain, the joinery, the maker's actual process photos instead of stock imagery. The store was built to let each piece breathe, with minimal chrome around the product photography.

Result

The new site became the studio's primary sales channel within four months, replacing word-of-mouth and craft fairs as the main source of orders.
