The brief

Field researchers working without reliable signal needed a way to log structured notes, photos, and GPS coordinates that would sync automatically once they were back online.

What we did

We built the app offline-first from the first line of code rather than bolting on sync later, with a queue system that made conflicts visible instead of silently resolving them.

Result

Released independently on the App Store, now used by research teams across three universities.
