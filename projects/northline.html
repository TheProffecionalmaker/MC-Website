The brief

Northline's dispatch team was tracking bus arrivals across a spreadsheet and three disconnected tools. They needed one live view that dispatchers and the public could both eventually use.

What we did

We designed a real-time map and schedule view built on their existing GPS feed, prioritizing the information dispatchers actually acted on — delays, gaps in coverage, and vehicles running early.

Result

Dispatch response time to service gaps improved, and the same dashboard later became the base for Northline's public-facing arrivals page.
