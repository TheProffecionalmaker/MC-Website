Harbor — booking platform redesign

A ground-up redesign of a marina booking system used by 40+ ports, cutting average checkout time from six minutes to ninety seconds.

The brief

Harbor's booking flow had grown port by port for six years with no one owning the whole experience. Slip owners were abandoning checkout at a high rate, and support tickets were dominated by the same handful of confusing steps.

What we did

We mapped the full booking flow end to end, cut it from eleven steps to four, and rebuilt the front end from scratch on a shared design system so every port's booking page finally looked and worked the same way.

Result

Average checkout time dropped from six minutes to ninety seconds, and support tickets related to booking fell by more than half in the first quarter after launch.
