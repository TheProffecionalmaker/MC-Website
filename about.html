About

Small on purpose.

We stayed two people on purpose. It means every project gets the same attention the first one did, and nothing gets handed off to someone who wasn't in the room when it started.

How we work

01

Few projects, more time

We cap it at six a year. That's the difference between a project we finished and a project we're still proud of two years later.

02

Design and build together

The person who designs the interface is the person who builds it. Nothing gets lost in a hand-off that never needed to happen.

03

Straight answers

If something won't work, we'll say so before you've paid for it, not after.
